01 / THE PROJECT

More parallel work.
Less data movement.

My goal is to parallelize the simulation of a quantum computer using GPUs, with the main focus on speeding up state-vector updates and scheduling the necessary computations as effectively as possible.

I will build the simulator from scratch in C++ and CUDA on the GHC machines, validate small circuits against Qiskit, and compare the GPU implementation with a sequential baseline.

01

Amplitude updates

Update independent amplitude pairs concurrently within each gate.

02

Gate scheduling

Explore fusion and separately simulate qubit groups when their states remain independent.

03

Multiple devices

A stretch goal: trade communication for a larger available state-vector memory.

REPORT · DEC 10 / POSTER · DEC 11

SUCCESS CRITERIA

Correctness first. Measured speedup.

The 100–250× speedup target is aspirational and measured against my own single-threaded CPU baseline. I will evaluate scaling across qubit counts and circuit lengths. A small Shor demonstration, such as factoring 119, depends on circuit feasibility; multi-device execution remains a stretch goal.
